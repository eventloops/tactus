### Method notes that bind the evidence below

- **Nothing was taken on trust from a prior record.** The first G4 report's measurements and its
  verification's derivations were not reused as evidence. Every trace, refusal pair, kill and count in
  this report was produced here at `81ee09ef`. The one thing carried over is the *text of 36 mutation
  anchors* from the first run's catalogue (which the first report itself designed from reading the
  tree); each anchor was re-verified to occur exactly once in the frozen tree, re-applied and
  re-measured here, against the **full** library suite rather than a subset, and twelve further
  mutations were designed here for the claims the verification found unwitnessed (§3).
- **Mutation is the detection test.** A green suite proves the tests pass, not that they still detect.
  Every "test X guards Y" claim below is backed by a mutation of Y observed to kill X here.
- **Where a claim was constructed rather than found, the construction is temporary and uncommitted,
  and where it makes an assertion it is a witness.** Eighteen measurement tests (`g4b_*`) were appended
  to `src/engine/topology/recover/tests.rs` for the run, together with a `three_tasks` flag on the
  recover fixture (so that a lineage can be created after another was released) and one `eprintln!`
  in each of the two kill samplers; the files were restored with `git checkout` and their hashes
  verified against `HEAD` before the report head was built (§1, re-run). Their source and every log
  are kept beside this report's evidence (`~/tactus-artifacts/g4b-evidence-81ee09ef/` on the build
  box, and the session scratchpad); their verbatim output is quoted below under the tag it printed
  (`G4A1` … `G4K-torn`). Three of them assert — the after-release ordering at the loop (`G4A3`), the
  overridden repair's exhaustion to the human rung (`G4C3`) and the two T-ANSWER kills (`G4K`) — so
  they were part of the detection set in §3; a kill by one of them alone is reported as such and
  not as a committed witness.
- **Every live/durable comparison is live against durable.** The verification of the first run found
  its Q1 helper comparing two replays. Here `live_equals_replay` compares the stepped `TopologyRun`'s
  own fold (`run.fold().state()`) with a fresh `TopologyFold::replay` of the bytes read back from
  `events.jsonl`; `replay_twice_equal` is printed beside it and is the weaker claim.
- **Every mutation and every measurement in this report is a Linux result.** All were applied and
  measured on this box (`x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, kernel 6.8.0-137-generic, cargo 1.97.1). None was
  re-run on the Windows guest or on macOS. The **unmutated** tree is green on all three: at the frozen
  sha the commit's own check runs report `test (ubuntu-latest)`, `test (macos-latest)` and
  `test (winguest)` success, `lint` and `msrv` green on ubuntu, macos and windows, in both the
  merge-group run that landed #256 (`34374716814`) and the `push` run on `master` at the same sha
  (`34375154938`); the newest run per check name was taken, queried through the GitHub API at 16:19Z
  (§1). What the mutations bound is *detection*, and the mechanisms they probe — the fold's refusals,
  the queue's eligibility rule, the lease table's counter — are platform-independent by construction.
- **Verify by hash, not by line count.** The mutation driver asserts each anchor occurs exactly once,
  that each mutated file's hash equals `HEAD`'s before the mutation and differs after it, and that it
  equals `HEAD`'s again after `git checkout` before the next mutation.
